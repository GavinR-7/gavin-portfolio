## Context

A tent, inflatable and party rental business on Long Island needed a site that customers could use on a phone to work out what size tent fits their guest count and ask for a quote.

## What I built

- A five page mobile first site: tents, inflatables, mechanical bull, tables and chairs, and contact.
- A tent sizing calculator built from the client's own rules: one round table per 100 sq ft and 10 chairs per table, combining tents above 80 guests.
- All content (copy, photos, phone numbers, inventory and the sizing chart) in one typed data file, so changing a price or phone number is a data edit, not a code change. Items with no price show "Call for pricing".
- Built with Next.js 15 (App Router), TypeScript and Tailwind CSS v4, and handed over on the client's own domain.

## Spam protection on the quote form

I added layered protection in two reviewed pull requests:

- A hidden honeypot field and a 1.5 second minimum fill time trap. Both drop a submission silently.
- A reCAPTCHA v2 checkbox, checked on the server.
- All checks run on the server, so the browser is never trusted.
- Dropped submissions log a reason and a timestamp only, never the form contents.
- A graceful fallback: if the captcha keys are unset or Google is unreachable, real leads still get through.

## Status and honesty

Both pull requests are merged. I have no measured spam reduction yet, so this page makes no claim about how much spam was blocked. Before and after figures will be added after a corrected Resend report. No traffic or conversion numbers are claimed.
